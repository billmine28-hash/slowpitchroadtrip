About this tournament

The Southwest Championships is a senior slowpitch tournament run by Senior Softball-USA, in Las Vegas, Nevada, running Tuesday, April 6 to Sunday, April 11, 2027. Divisions, entry fees, field sites and registration are posted on the official SSUSA event page linked above. That page is the authority if anything here differs.

Registration and details are on the event's SSUSA page, or call the Senior Softball-USA office at 916-326-5303.
