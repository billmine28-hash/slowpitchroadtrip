About this tournament

The Spring World Championships is a senior slowpitch tournament run by Senior Softball-USA, in St. George, Utah, running Tuesday, March 16 to Sunday, March 21, 2027. Registration and details are on the event's SSUSA page, or call the Senior Softball-USA office at 916-326-5303.
