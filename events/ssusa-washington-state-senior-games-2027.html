About this tournament

The Washington State Senior Games is a senior slowpitch tournament run by Senior Softball-USA, in Shelton, Washington, running Thursday, September 9 to Sunday, September 12, 2027. Divisions, entry fees, field sites and registration are posted on the official SSUSA event page linked above. That page is the authority if anything here differs.

Registration and details are on the event's SSUSA page, or call the Senior Softball-USA office at 916-326-5303.
