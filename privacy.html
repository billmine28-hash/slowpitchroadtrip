Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Slowpitch Road Trip is an independent website for senior slowpitch players. This page says what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Questions and requests go to slowpitchroadtrip@yahoo.com.

What you give us

  • Email list: your email address, when you sign up for updates. Every update email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Free agent listings: name, city, state, age division, positions, travel note, any note you add, and email address. After review, your first name and last initial and the other details, including your email, are shown publicly.
  • Team listings: team name, city, state, division, positions needed, tournaments, any note, and a contact email. After review these are shown publicly.
  • Feedback: your message, and your name and email if you choose to include them.
  • Team trip answers: the first name you type and your in, maybe or can't answer. Anyone who has your team's trip link can see them.
  • Photos and emails: anything you email us. We look at every photo before it goes up.

What stays on your own device

Your season tracker games, saved tournaments and some page settings are kept in your browser. We do not receive your season tracker. Clearing your browser data erases them.

ZIP codes and location

If you type a ZIP code or place to find tournaments or weather, it is sent to a lookup service (Zippopotam.us, and Open-Meteo for weather) to get map coordinates and forecasts. The tournament finder only uses your device location if you allow it, to work out distances.

Other companies involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and, like any web host, receives your IP address.
  • Supabase stores listings, feedback and trip answers.
  • Kit runs the email list.
  • GoatCounter counts visits. It does not use cookies.
  • Expedia Group supplies the travel banners on our home page. Those banners load from Expedia, which may collect information or set cookies under its own policy.
  • YouTube supplies preview pictures and videos on some pages.
  • Ko-fi, hotel sites, bat sellers, maps and organizer pages are separate websites. Their own policies apply once you follow a link.

How we use information

To run the features you use, answer you, send the updates you asked for, and keep out spam. We do not sell your information.

Tracking and Do Not Track

We do not follow you across other websites, and the site does not change its behavior for browser "Do Not Track" signals. Outside services listed above may collect information under their own policies.

How long we keep it

Until it is no longer needed or you ask us to remove it.

Change or remove your information

Email slowpitchroadtrip@yahoo.com to see, correct or delete your listing, feedback, trip answers or email sign-up. Please write from the address you used, or tell us enough to find the entry.

Age

The site is written for adult players. It is not directed to children.

Changes

When this policy changes we update this page and the date above.
