Get your senior team sponsored

What we're trying to do: tournament entries often run several hundred dollars a weekend, and on top of that come uniforms and travel. For a lot of senior teams, that all comes out of the players' own pockets. We want to help by connecting teams with local businesses and national brands that want to reach senior players, so the sponsor helps cover the costs and gets its name on your jerseys and banner.

This is a first try for the 2027 season, so we want to see how many teams are interested before we start asking sponsors. Sponsor money goes straight to what your team needs: uniforms, tournament entry fees or a team banner. It's paid directly for those things, so there's no cash for anyone to handle.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your team below. It takes about two minutes.
  2. We'll reach out to local businesses and national brands and try to match them with teams on the list.
  3. If a sponsor picks your team, we'll contact you before anything is set. You wear their logo and fly their banner at tournaments.

This is an interest list, not a promise of sponsorship. Signing up is free and doesn't commit your team to anything. Sign up by February 15, 2027.
